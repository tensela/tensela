## What for

To know at a glance where to look for a result: in the **3D view** or **on the card**.

## The two kinds

![A node that makes geometry and a node that only calculates](/images/manual/diagrams/two-kinds-en.svg)

| | Make geometry | Only calculate |
|---|---|---|
| **Examples** | Rectangle, Grid, Loft, Place, Ribs | Area, Volume, Distance, Formula, Number, Info |
| **Where you see the result** | in the 3D view | on the card |
| **On the card** | an eye to show or hide it, colour, opacity, numbering, direction | an ⓘ that unfolds the values on the card |

## Tips

- **Turn eyes off** to see only what matters: often you only want to see the last node of the chain.
- **Info** shows what any cable carries, as numbers or as a list, without changing anything.
- **Paint by a value:** to colour panels by their area or by the sun they get, use the panel of the geometry
  (**Colour → by a value**). It is not a separate node.
