You do not need any of this to design with Tensela. These pages are for whoever wants to know where things come
from, and they come in two parts.

## Designing with rules

The ideas and the mathematics of parametric design, which are the same in any tool:

- [Visual algorithms](/manual/theory/visual-algorithms/): the algorithm as a recipe, graphs of nodes, and where
  parametric design comes from.
- [Units and directions](/manual/theory/units-and-directions/): metres, degrees, north and time.
- [NURBS curves and surfaces](/manual/theory/nurbs/): control points, degree, weights and knots.
- [Curvature and developable surfaces](/manual/theory/curvature/): flat sheet or moulded.
- [Meshes](/manual/theory/meshes/): geometry, topology, flat faces and subdivision.
- [Form finding and the best value](/manual/theory/form-finding/): how Equilibrium and *Find the best* work.

## Inside Tensela

How this program in particular works, with diagrams and some code:

- [How Tensela thinks](/manual/theory/how-tensela-thinks/): blocks of numbers with named levels.
- [Named lists, inside](/manual/theory/named-lists-inside/): how a list is kept and the one rule that combines them.
- [How a project is calculated](/manual/theory/the-engine/): the graph, what is recalculated, and the limits.
- [Lists in Tensela, Grasshopper and Dynamo](/manual/theory/lists-compared/): the same tasks in the three.
