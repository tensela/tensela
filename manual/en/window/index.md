The window has two main areas and a few panels around them.

![The window of Tensela: the 3D view, the canvas with the first project, and the panels](/images/manual/app/window-en.png)

| Area | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The canvas** | where the nodes and cables are. See [The canvas](/manual/window/canvas/). |
| **The 3D view** | what the nodes make, updated as you work. See [The 3D view](/manual/window/view/). |
| **The panels** | *Node* (the settings of the chosen node), *Values* (sliders), *Data*, *Nodes*, *Library* and *Croquis*. See [The panels](/manual/window/panels/). |
| **The tabs** | each open project is a tab, like in a browser: right-click a tab to close, rename, duplicate or find its file. |

## Arranging the window

- Panels can be dragged by their title, stacked as tabs, or floated. **View → Panels** shows or hides each one.
- **View → Workspaces** saves the current arrangement with a name, to come back to it; **View → Workspaces →
  Factory layout** puts everything back as it came.
- **View → Mode** switches between light and dark; **Preferences** has the rest of the appearance.
  See [Preferences](/manual/window/preferences/).

## The menus at a glance

| Menu | What is there |
|---|---|
| **File** | new, open, recent, save, close; **Export** to images, drawings, 3D, 3D printing and cutting; **Open in** another program |
| **Edit** | undo and redo, add a node, find a node, copy and paste, group, **Find the best…**, **Preferences** |
| **View** | frame, isolate, show and hide nodes, arrange and align nodes, the grid and guides, colour legends, panels, workspaces, mode |
| **Help** | the manual (without internet), help for the chosen node (**F1**), shortcuts, the PDF, community, report a problem, support Tensela, **About Tensela** |

## Help

**Help → Manual** opens this manual from your computer, without internet. With a node chosen, **F1** opens it at the part about that node. **Help → About Tensela** shows its version, its licence, the people to thank and the libraries it is built with.
