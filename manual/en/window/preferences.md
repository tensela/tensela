**Edit → Preferences…** (Ctrl + ,). Changes show at once and are remembered.

![Preferences, General section](/images/manual/app/preferences-en.png)

| Section | What you set |
|---|---|
| **General** | the **language** (English or Español; it changes when Tensela is opened again); the **mode**, light, dark or like the system; the **location** where a new Sun, Insolation or Daylight starts |
| **3D view** | the **style** on opening, the **background**, whether to show the axes |
| **Nodes** | how the **node cards** are drawn: neutral with a strip of colour, or the header fully coloured; the **canvas** background and pattern (dots, square grid or plain), the grid and the guides |
| **Units** | the unit **lengths** are shown in (m, cm, mm, feet or inches), the **decimals**, and the inch fraction |
| **Interaction** | the **mouse** scheme of the 3D view (Tensela, Blender, Rhino or SketchUp), where the wheel **zooms**, and the **keyboard shortcuts**: click one and press the new keys |
| **Code editor** | the colours, the letter and its size, and the helps while writing in a **Code** node |

## Units

You can type a length in **any unit**, `120 cm`, `4'`, `6 1/2"`, and a plain number is read in the unit you chose.
The project is always saved in metres, so it opens the same for anyone.
