## Node

The settings of the chosen node: each input with its value, its options and its help, and, for geometry, its colour,
opacity and numbering.

- **More settings** opens the inputs that few people need: the defaults work almost always.
- With several nodes chosen, a value typed here goes to all of them; dragging up or down over a number changes them
  all at once.
- Some nodes add their own tools here: the graph of a **Graph**, the editor of a **NURBS Curve** (*Edit curve*), the
  **Move supports** of an **Equilibrium**, the report of a **Print check** or a **Lay flat**.

## Values

The **sliders** of the project, all together: the numbers you want to play with, without looking for their nodes. A
value of any node becomes a slider with **Show this value as a slider in “Values”**, in the Node panel. Each slider
keeps its range and step.

## Data

The values of the chosen node as a table: every element with its numbers, along its levels. **Save as CSV…** takes
them to a spreadsheet; **Copy as text** to anywhere.

## Nodes

The list of every node of the project, with its kind, its family, what it gives and whether it is up to date. A click
chooses it on the canvas: handy in large projects.

## Library

Your own groups, saved with **Save to library…**, to use in any project, as a list or as a mosaic. **Ctrl + Alt + L**
shows or hides it. **Edit → Open the library folder** opens where they are kept.

## Croquis

The 3D view drawn as a freehand architect's sketch: ink, marker, watercolour, hatching. It follows the view as you turn
it, and **File → Export → Freehand sketch** saves it as PNG, SVG or PDF.
