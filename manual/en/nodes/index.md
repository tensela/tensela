---
title: All the nodes
description: Every node of Tensela, by family.
---

Each family has its colour on the canvas. Each page tells every node in the same order: **what for**, **how**, **what
it does** (each input in plain words) and **tips**.

See them all at once on the [map of all the nodes](/manual/nodes/map/).

| Family | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [Values](/manual/nodes/values/) | numbers, lists, formulas and switches that drive the design |
| [Shapes](/manual/nodes/shapes/) | rectangles, boxes, prisms, spheres and points to start from |
| [Curves](/manual/nodes/curves/) | lines, arcs, circles, catenaries, free curves, and how to divide, cut and label them |
| [Surfaces](/manual/nodes/surfaces/) | surfaces through curves, points or edges, structures on surfaces, unrolling |
| [Divide](/manual/nodes/divide/) | grids, Voronoi, faces, vertices, and picking, splitting and reordering lists |
| [Measure](/manual/nodes/measure/) | distances, attractors, remapping, areas and volumes, conditions |
| [Transform](/manual/nodes/transform/) | move, place, orient, rotate, scale, twist, morph, thicken, frame |
| [Meshes](/manual/nodes/meshes/) | meshes from surfaces, points or triangles, joining, smoothing, editing by hand |
| [Fabrication](/manual/nodes/fabrication/) | sections, slots, plates, flat panels, sheets with numbers, bevels, print check |
| [Environment](/manual/nodes/environment/) | sun, shadow, incidence, insolation, daylight, climate |
| [Simulation](/manual/nodes/simulation/) | hanging shapes, bar analysis, topology optimization |
| [Groups](/manual/nodes/groups/) | the group result, and the nodes inside a group |
