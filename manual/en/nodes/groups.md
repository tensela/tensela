How to group nodes, go inside a group, repeat it, or nest groups inside groups is told in
[Groups](/manual/key-ideas/groups/), among the key ideas. This page is about the one node of the family.

## Group result

**What for.** Another result of a group that gives several. The card of the group gives the first one; each Group
result takes one more, **without computing the group again**.

**How.** It appears by itself when you group nodes and two of them are used outside: next to the group card, with
the name of the node it replaces. To take one more by hand, connect the group card to **group** and write in
**gives** the name of the result you want.

| Input | What it means |
|---|---|
| **group** | the card of a group with several results |
| **gives** | which result: the name of one of its *Group output* nodes |

**Tips.**
- Why one card per result: a cable carries one thing. The group card gives the first result, and each Group result
  gives another.

## Inside a group

Inside a group, the search offers two more kinds of node, which only exist there:

- **Group input:** what reaches the group from outside, one per input of its card.
- **Group output:** what leaves the group. Connect to it the result; add more Group outputs, each with its name,
  for a group that gives several.
