A 24 × 12 m facade of 128 frames. Near a point the openings are wide; far from it they almost close. Move the point and the facade breathes with it.

**5 nodes** · [Download `breathing-facade.tela`](/examples/recipes/breathing-facade.tela)

## Step by step

<Steps>

1. **The facade.** Add a **Rectangle**: **width** `24`, **height** `12`, **plane** *elevation*.

2. **The panels.** Add a **Grid** and connect the Rectangle to **rectangle**. Write **columns** `16` and **rows** `8`: 128 panels of 1.5 m.

3. **The point.** Add a **Point** with **x** `8` and **z** `6`.

4. **How open each panel is.** Add an **Attractor**. Connect the Grid to **where** and the Point to **attractors**. Write **reach** `10`, **near** `0.85` and **far** `0.15`.

5. **The frames.** Add a **Frame**. Connect the Grid to **panel** and the Attractor to **opening**. Write **thickness** `0.12`.

</Steps>

## Play with it

- Move the **Point** across the facade.
- Swap **near** and **far**: closed around the point, open everywhere else.
- Add a second Point and connect it to **more** of the Attractor.
