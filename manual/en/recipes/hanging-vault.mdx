A 12 × 12 m membrane held at its four corners hangs under its own weight. Turned upside down, that shape is a vault that works mainly in compression: the method of the hanging models of Gaudí and Isler.

It is a form study, not a structural calculation: an engineer has to check it before it is built.

**3 nodes** · [Download `hanging-vault.tela`](/examples/recipes/hanging-vault.tela)

## Step by step

<Steps>

1. **The plan.** Add a **Rectangle**: **width** `12`, **height** `12`.

2. **The membrane.** Add a **Mesh surface** with **count u** `16` and **count v** `16`.

3. **The vault.** Add an **Equilibrium**: the mesh to **shape**, **self weight** `300`, **gravity** `0 0 1` (upwards: the hanging shape turned over).

</Steps>

## Play with it

- Change **supports** to *border*: a vault resting on its four edges.
- Add **more supports** inside: a vault on columns.
- Set **show** to *forces* to see the compression in each span.
