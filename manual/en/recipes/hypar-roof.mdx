An 18 × 12 m roof at 4 m in six bays. Each bay rises at two opposite corners and becomes a hyperbolic paraboloid, a saddle made only of straight lines, like the shells of Félix Candela.

**6 nodes** · [Download `hypar-roof.tela`](/examples/recipes/hypar-roof.tela)

## Step by step

<Steps>

1. **The roof plan.** Add a **Rectangle**: **width** `18`, **height** `12`, **origin** `0 0 4`.

2. **The bays.** Add a **Grid** with **columns** `3` and **rows** `2`.

3. **The saddles.** Add a **Hypar**: the Grid to **panels**, **lift** `1.5`.

4. **Where the columns go.** Add **Vertices** with the Grid: the corners of every bay.

5. **The columns.** Add a **Line by direction** from the corners, **direction** `0 0 -1`, **length** `4`, and a **Pipe** around those lines with **radius** `0.15`.

</Steps>

## Play with it

- Change the **lift**: flat at `0`, dramatic at `3`.
- Choose the other two **corners** to rise.
- With **show** *flat piece*, each bay opened along its fold into two triangles, to cut it from a sheet for a model.
