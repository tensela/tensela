A 14 × 10 m building, 30 m tall, in Montevideo on the 21st of June, the winter solstice there: where its shadow falls at 10, 12 and 14 h, and how many hours of sun each panel of its faces, roof included, gets over the winter.

**6 nodes** · [Download `sun-study.tela`](/examples/recipes/sun-study.tela)

## Step by step

<Steps>

1. **The building.** Add a **Prism**: **width** `14`, **depth** `10`, **height** `30`.

2. **Its panels.** Add **Faces** with **which** *all faces* (the façades, the roof and the base), and a **Grid** on them with **columns** `7` and **rows** `15`.

3. **The sun.** Add a **Sun**: **location** *Montevideo*, **month** `6`, **day** `21`, **hour** `10; 12; 14`, **show** *ray*.

4. **The shadows.** Add a **Shadow**: the Prism to **geometry**, the Sun to **ray**.

5. **The sun hours.** Add an **Insolation**: the Grid to **faces**, **location** *Montevideo*, **period** *winter*, **show** *sun hours*. Paint the Grid by this value in its panel (**Colour → by a value**).

</Steps>

## Play with it

- Press the ▶ of the hour in the Sun to see the shadow move through the day.
- Add the neighbouring buildings to **obstructions** of the Insolation.
- Change **period** to *summer* and compare the north and south façades.
