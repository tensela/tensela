A 12 × 8 m facade covered with 60 irregular panels, each one a Voronoi cell, shrunk a little to leave an open joint, and given a thickness.

**5 nodes** · [Download `voronoi-skin.tela`](/examples/recipes/voronoi-skin.tela)

## Step by step

<Steps>

1. **The facade.** Add a **Rectangle**: **width** `12`, **height** `8`, **plane** *elevation*.

2. **The seeds.** Add **Random points** with the Rectangle as **region**, **count** `60`.

3. **The cells.** Add a **Voronoi**: the points to **points**, the Rectangle to **boundary**.

4. **The joints.** Add a **Scale** with **factor** `0.88`: each cell shrinks about its own centre.

5. **The panels.** Add a **Thicken** with **thickness** `0.12`.

</Steps>

## Play with it

- Try other values of **seed** in Random points until you like the pattern.
- More **count**, smaller cells.
- Drive the **factor** of Scale with an **Attractor**: wider joints near a point.
