A tower of 24 floors, 3.5 m apart. Every floor is the same rounded plan, turned 6° more than the one below: 138° from the ground to the top.

**7 nodes** · [Download `twisting-tower.tela`](/examples/recipes/twisting-tower.tela)

## Step by step

<Steps>

1. **The floors.** Add a **Series**: **count** `24`, step `1`, **level** `floor`.

2. **Their heights.** Add a **Formula** `a * 3.5` with the Series as **a**.

3. **Their turns.** Add another **Formula** `a * 6` with the Series as **a**: degrees for each floor.

4. **The plan.** Add a **Rounded rectangle**: **width** `24`, **height** `16`, **radius** `4`, **origin** `-12 -8 0` (centred on the axis).

5. **Up and around.** Add a **Move** with the heights as **distance**, then a **Rotate** with the turns as **degrees**.

6. **The slabs.** Add an **Extrude** with **vector** `0 0 0.35` and **caps** on.

</Steps>

## Play with it

- Change the `6` of the turns: a calm tower or a spiral.
- Make the turn grow towards the top with `a * a * 0.25`.
- Scale each floor with a third Formula for a tower that tapers.
